Reelcards demo

The whole student journey in a browser, running the same engine as the Android app. Set up on the phone, then scroll: flashcard reels slip in between normal reels, styled for the app you're in (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) and the things you watch. The panels explain every decision. The design lab at the bottom shows every card style side by side.

Get Reelcards

Pick your device. Each one takes about a minute. Not sure yet? Try it right here first: the phone below is the real thing, running in your browser.

For your phone

Android phone

The full app. Cards slip in between your real videos in TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat, Facebook, X, Reddit and Pinterest.

Download the app (.apk)
  1. Tap the button. If the phone warns the file can harm your device, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install. If Play Protect says it hasn't seen it, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  3. Open Reelcards and tap Set up in 1 minute. Pick a deck and your exam date.
  4. Tap Switch it on, agree, and turn on Reelcards in Accessibility. Switch greyed out? Go back, tap Open app info, then ⋮, then Allow restricted settings, and try again.
  5. Scroll TikTok, Instagram or YouTube Shorts. A card appears every few videos.

Early test version: it hasn't run on many phones yet. All versions

For your phone

iPhone

iPhones don't let any app draw over TikTok, so a card can't slip in by itself. This gives you the app and a one-tap card.

  1. Open this page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Set up your decks in it.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, Add Action, then Open URLs. Paste this link:
  3. Name it Reelcards, then add it to your Home Screen, Action Button or Back Tap. Tap it when you want a card.

Optional: in Shortcuts, Automation, App, pick TikTok and Is Opened, then Run Immediately, add Wait 5 minutes and the same Open URLs. Untested on a real iPhone. Use the home-screen copy or Safari for the app, not both: they keep separate cards.

For your computer

Computer

A browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera. Cards slip in between videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X.

Download the extension (.zip)
  1. Unzip the file. You get a folder called Reelcards-extension; keep it somewhere safe, because Chrome reads it from there.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin Reelcards from the puzzle-piece menu, click it, pick a deck and open one of the sites.

Not in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's installed this way.
