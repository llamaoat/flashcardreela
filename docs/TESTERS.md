# Trying Reelcards: a guide for testers

Thank you for testing. Reelcards slips flashcards in between the videos you scroll on TikTok,
Instagram, YouTube Shorts and other apps, drawn so they look like another video.

**This is an early test version. It has not been tried on a real phone by its makers yet**, so
you are one of the first. Things may break. That is what we want to find out.

**The quickest way:** open **https://llamaoat.github.io/flashcardreela/**. The "Get Reelcards" box at the
top has the download button and the steps for Android, iPhone and computer, with the one for your
device highlighted. The rest of this page says the same in more detail.

## What you need

- An **Android phone** (Android 8 or newer). The real thing only works on Android.
- Or an **iPhone or a computer**: see the last section.

## Install it on Android

1. On your phone, open **https://github.com/llamaoat/flashcardreela/releases** and tap the newest
   release (the top one).
2. Under **Assets**, tap **Reelcards.apk**. Your phone may warn that this type of file can harm your
   device. Tap **Download anyway** (or **OK**). It is a normal app file.
3. When it has downloaded, tap **Open** (or find it in your **Downloads** or **Files** app and tap it).
4. If Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps, tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow
   from this source**, press back, then tap **Install**.
5. If **Google Play Protect** says it hasn't seen this app, tap **More details**, then **Install
   anyway**. It says this about every app that isn't from the Play Store.
6. Tap **Open**.

## Set it up (about a minute)

1. Tap **Set up in 1 minute**.
2. **Pick what you're revising:** a ready-made deck, your own notes, or an Anki file. Tap **Next**.
3. Choose your exam date and how often you want cards. For testing, pick **Demo**, which shows a
   card every two videos.
4. **Switch it on:** tap the button, read the note and tap **Agree and open settings**. In
   Accessibility, find **Reelcards** (it may be under *Installed apps* or *Downloaded apps*), turn it
   on and confirm.
   - **The switch is greyed out?** Android 13 and newer block this for apps installed from a file. Go
     back to Reelcards, tap **Open app info**, tap **⋮** (top right), choose **Allow restricted
     settings**, confirm, then try the switch again.
5. Tap **Show me a test card** to see what a card looks like.

Reelcards only watches for you swiping to the next video. It does not read your messages, and it
does not save or send what you watch. There is no account and no internet access.

## Use it

1. Open TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat, Facebook, X, Reddit or Pinterest and scroll as
   normal.
2. After a few videos, a card appears on top, drawn like the app you're in.
   - **Tap** to see the answer.
   - **Double-tap** if you knew it.
   - **Swipe up** to carry on.
3. Open Reelcards any time for today's numbers. On the dashboard, **How often** changes how many
   cards you get.
4. Optional: long-press your home screen, choose **Widgets**, and add **Reelcards** for a card on
   your home screen.

## What we'd most like to know

Try each of these and note whether it worked:

- Did a card appear between videos in each app you use? Which ones didn't?
- Did the card sit nicely, or did it cover buttons or text?
- Did tapping, double-tapping and swiping work?
- Did the card ever appear when it shouldn't (for example in messages), or stop videos oddly?
- Did the app feel good, light or dark? Did the moving sea on the first screen run smoothly?
- Anything confusing, slow or ugly.

## Tell us what happened

Please send:

- Your **phone** and **Android version** (Settings → About phone).
- The **app** you were in (TikTok, Instagram and so on).
- What you **expected** and what **happened**. A screen recording or screenshot helps a lot.
- In Reelcards, open the dashboard and scroll to **Reel detection log**. Copy or screenshot it if
  something didn't appear.

Send it to whoever gave you this link.

## If something goes wrong

- **Cards never appear:** check that Reelcards is still switched on under Accessibility. Some phones
  switch it off to save battery; if so, switch it back on and set Reelcards to *unrestricted* battery.
- **To stop:** turn Reelcards off under Accessibility, or uninstall it like any app. Your cards live
  only on your phone and go with it.

## iPhone or computer

- **iPhone:** open **https://llamaoat.github.io/flashcardreela/** in Safari. The page shows how to
  add it to your home screen and how to make a Shortcut that opens straight onto a card. An iPhone
  can't put cards inside TikTok itself.
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** the browser extension is in the **Actions** tab on GitHub (open the
  newest *Build* run, download **Reelcards-extension**, unzip it). Instructions are in
  [`extension/README.md`](../extension/README.md).
