Let it on the video sites

Safari asks before any extension works on a website. Reelcards only works on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and X, and only notices which video is playing.

No box came up? Open YouTube in Safari, tap the ⋯ More button, tap Reelcards and choose Always Allow. Then come back here and check again.
